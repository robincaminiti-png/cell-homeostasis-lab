Cell Homeostasis Virtual Lab

Purpose: To observe cell homeostasis at work by modeling a cell with dialysis tubing placed in solutions containing different concentrations of sugar.

Use this simulation with your Cell Homeostasis Lab worksheet. Record information on your worksheet as you move through each step. Do not skip ahead—the goal is to make predictions before seeing the results.

1. Understand the Model

Dialysis tubing is selectively permeable, so it can model a cell membrane. In this investigation, the liquid inside the dialysis tubing represents the fluid inside a cell, while the liquid in the beaker represents the environment outside the cell.

Beaker A is the control: there is no sugar inside or outside the dialysis tube.

5. Analyze What Happened

Use the data you recorded to complete the Data Analysis section of your worksheet. Before you finish, check your reasoning with these prompts.

Think about: Why did B and C gain mass? Why did E lose mass? Why did D have very little change? Relate each observation to the direction of water movement across the selectively permeable membrane.

Finish your worksheet: Identify the independent and dependent variables, list four controlled variables, classify the solutions as hypertonic/isotonic/hypotonic, and explain the tube(s) with little or no mass change.
